About the Conference

Where Open Science Shapes the Future

The UNIMAS Open Science Conference brings together researchers, policymakers, and innovators to advance the principles of making scientific research and data accessible to all. Hosted by Universiti Malaysia Sarawak, this landmark event is co-funded by the European Union under the Open Asia initiative.

Our mission is to enable Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable (FAIR) research data, reinforce open scientific inquiry and integrity, enable better research management, and promote data-intensive research across the globe.
